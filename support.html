How to play

Moving your line
Drag anywhere on the screen. The line follows how far your finger travels, so a tap on its own does nothing.
Winning
First to 11 points. Let the dot past your line and your opponent scores.
Perks
Perks drop onto the field mid-rally. Hit one with the ball to claim it — whoever last struck that ball takes it. There are four: Split Shot, Dampener, Squeeze and Booster. The pause menu has a HOW TO PLAY page explaining each one.
Sound and haptics
Both can be turned off in the pause menu, and the haptic strength adjusted. Sound follows your phone's silent switch.

Common questions

I can't hear anything
Check the silent switch on the side of your phone, and that SOUND is ON in the pause menu.
I can't feel the haptics
Check HAPTICS in the pause menu, and that System Haptics is enabled in Settings → Sounds & Haptics.
The game doesn't rotate
That's deliberate — the field stays put so play is consistent, but the score, timer and menus rotate to stay readable.
