Privacy Policy — Dot & Line

Last updated: 5 September 2026

Dot & Line does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party services of any kind.

What the app stores

The app saves your sound and haptic preferences on your device so they persist between sessions. This information stays on your device, is not transmitted anywhere, and is removed when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal information.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not contain advertising or analytics software.
  • It does not connect to the internet. The app contains no networking code.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects nothing from children. It is suitable for all ages.
